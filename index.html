Document Checklist

  • ✅ Class 10 marksheet / certificate
  • ✅ Class 12 marksheet / certificate
  • ✅ Latest semester marksheet (for renewal)
  • ✅ Entrance test scorecard / rank letter
  • ✅ Admission letter / bonafide certificate / college ID
  • ✅ Current year tuition fee receipt & official fee structure
  • ✅ Gap certificate (if applicable)
  • ✅ Course duration & institute recognition proof
  • ✅ Aadhaar card (masked copy where permitted)
  • ✅ Domicile / residence certificate
  • ✅ Jan Aadhaar (for Rajasthan schemes)
  • ✅ Bank passbook / cancelled cheque (IFSC visible)
  • ✅ Recent photograph & signature
  • ✅ PAN (where required by lender)
  • ✅ UDID card (for PwD applicants)
  • ✅ Passport (for overseas study)
  • ✅ Family income certificate (issued by competent authority)
  • ✅ Salary slip / employer certificate / ITR / pension proof
  • ✅ Caste/category certificate (SC/ST/OBC/SBC/EBC/DNT)
  • ✅ EWS certificate (where applicable)
  • ✅ Minority certificate/declaration (where required)
  • ✅ Disability certificate / UDID
  • ✅ Death certificate (for orphan / martyr ward)
  • ✅ Ex-serviceman/CAPF proof (if applying under service-ward scheme)
  • ✅ Signed loan application form + photographs
  • ✅ Student KYC: PAN, Aadhaar, address proof
  • ✅ Co-borrower (parent/guardian) KYC & income proof
  • ✅ Last 6 months bank statements
  • ✅ Scholarship / assistantship letter (if any)
  • ✅ Asset-liability statement (if required)
  • ✅ Collateral title deeds / valuation (if required)
  • ✅ Overseas: passport, offer letter, university cost estimate, visa

How to Apply (NSP)

  1. OTR Registration – Visit scholarships.gov.in → Register with Aadhaar → Get 14-digit OTR ID
  2. Select Scheme – Browse All-Scholarships → Open scheme card → Read eligibility & deadline carefully
  3. Fill Application – Login with OTR ID → Fill academic, bank, income details
  4. Upload Documents – JPG/PDF under size limit → Marksheets, certificate, income proof
  5. Institute Verification – Submit at college scholarship office before institute deadline
  6. Track Status – Login to NSP → Application Status; payment via PFMS → pfms.nic.in
💡 Tip: Students with disabilities must give UDID data-sharing consent on the UDID portal before applying on NSP.
🏜️

Rajasthan SSO Guide

  1. Create/recover SSO ID at sso.rajasthan.gov.in
  2. Ensure Jan Aadhaar family data is current (janaadhaar.rajasthan.gov.in)
  3. Login to SSO → Open Scholarship / SJE / College Education service
  4. Select: Academic year → Department → Scheme → Course → Year
  5. Upload JPG/PDF scans (usually ≤512 KB each)
  6. Submit → Save application number → Check objections regularly
  7. Submit physical documents to college nodal office before the institute deadline
🔑 Forgot SSO ID? SMS RJ SSO to 9223166166 from your registered mobile.

Loan Glossary (Plain Language)

ScholarshipMoney given to you — no repayment if you follow the rules.
Education LoanBorrowed money — you MUST repay principal + interest as per bank contract.
Interest SubsidyGovt pays some interest for a defined period. Principal repayment is still yours.
Credit GuaranteeGovt protects the bank, NOT you. You still must repay.
CollateralAsset (like property) pledged to the bank as security.
Co-BorrowerParent / guardian who shares legal repayment responsibility with you.
MarginPortion of course cost you/family pay from your own pocket.
MoratoriumBreak period (course + extra months) before EMI starts. Interest still accrues!
Floating RateYour EMI can go up or down as the benchmark rate changes.
Processing FeeBank's one-time charge for processing your loan. Check GST separately.

Fraud Prevention — Stay Safe!

  • 🚫 Never pay anyone promising guaranteed scholarship selection or faster NSP approval
  • 🚫 Never share OTP, UPI PIN, ATM PIN, net-banking password with anyone
  • 🚫 Never send full Aadhaar or bank credentials to unofficial WhatsApp numbers
  • ✅ Check domain carefully — official govt sites use .gov.in
  • ✅ Report fraud to Buddy4Study: reportfraud@buddy4study.com
  • ✅ Cybercrime helpline: 1930 | cybercrime.gov.in
  • ✅ Keep application number, receipt & email trail safely
